Privacy and terms

Kona is a browser music player developed by an individual and published free of charge. This document applies to the public build at wentolia.github.io/kona-open and its source code.

What is not collected

  • Kona has no server and no analytics. The developer receives none of your music, listening history, settings, name or location.
  • Settings, listening history and track information are stored only in your browser's localStorage, IndexedDB and Cache Storage. They can be exported and erased under Settings → Other → Data.

Location

  • The browser's location is used once a day, and only when Local keepsakes is turned on.
  • Coordinates are converted on the device to a prefecture or country code and discarded immediately. They are neither stored nor sent.
  • To determine location, the browser or operating system may use Google or Apple location services. That is governed by the browser and the operating system.

Voice

  • The microphone is used only when the host allows voice and you turn your microphone on.
  • Kona records neither voice nor music.
